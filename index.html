Stevie Workspace
PrivacyGitHub

A personal automation platform, self-hosted and run by one engineer.

Stevie Workspace connects my own accounts and everyday paperwork through workflows I design, deploy and operate myself.

What it does

Mail triage

Classifies and labels incoming messages in my own Gmail account.

Document handling

Retrieves attachments and sends replies when I ask for it.

Calendar

Reads and creates events on my own calendar.

How it is built

Containerized services with infrastructure kept as code, continuous delivery, monitoring, encrypted backups and centrally managed secrets.

Who can use it

It is a private tool, not a public service. It works only with my own Google account, and no sign-up is available. Read how data is handled in the privacy policy.
